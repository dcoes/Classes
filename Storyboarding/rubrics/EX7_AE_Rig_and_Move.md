# EX7 AE Rig & Move: Rubric
**Totals 100% of this assignment · 3% of the course grade · Band EX**
**Brief:** [`assignments/EX7_AE_Rig_and_Move.md`](../assignments/EX7_AE_Rig_and_Move.md)
**Assessed against CO1:** *Draw storyboards as a planning tool for their visual narratives.*

> A tool-fluency exercise. Its CO claim is deliberately thin and it is weighted accordingly. Near-free if attempted, zero if not.

---

## The five operations: 15% each, 75% total

Each is independently visible in the project file and scores on its own. That is why the `.aep` is a required deliverable: a student whose mask fails still earns most of the marks, and the failure is diagnosable in two minutes at the desk.

### 1 . PSD imported as a composition, layers intact: 15%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 15 | *Import As: Composition, Retain Layer Sizes.* Body, arm and background are separate layers in the timeline. |
| Good | 13 | Imported as Composition without Retain Layer Sizes. Layers present, anchor points awkward. |
| Average | 11 | Layers separated by hand after a flat import. |
| Developing | 8 | Imported as footage; one flat image. |
| Missing | 0 | No project file. |

### 2 . Anchor point moved to the shoulder, arm parented to body: 15%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 15 | Both halves. The arm rotates from the joint and is a child of the body. |
| Good | 13 | Parented, anchor point moved but not to the joint. |
| Average | 11 | Parented, anchor point untouched. The arm rotates around its own centre. |
| Developing | 8 | Anchor moved, no parenting. |
| Missing | 0 | Neither. |

**Check this one in the project file, not the render.** An arm rotating around its own middle often still reads as a wave on playback. It is also the conceptual core of the exercise: an anchor point is a joint, and that idea carries into every rig these students touch afterward.

### 3 . Body animated toward camera: 15%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 15 | Position and scale keyframed. The arm follows without separate position keys of its own. |
| Good | 13 | Animated, with one redundant key on the arm. |
| Average | 11 | Position only, no scale, so the approach doesn't read. |
| Developing | 8 | Arm keyframed separately in position instead of following the parent. |
| Missing | 0 | No movement. |

### 4 . Wave, rotation keys on the parented arm: 15%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 15 | Three or more rotation keyframes on the arm layer. |
| Good | 13 | Two keys. The wave reads as a single motion. |
| Average | 11 | Animated on a property other than rotation. |
| Developing | 8 | One key, or the arm moves with the body only. |
| Missing | 0 | No wave. |

### 5 . Exit into the house via a background mask: 15%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 15 | A masked copy of the background sits in front of the character, who passes behind the doorway. |
| Good | 13 | Mask present and slightly off, so an edge shows. |
| Average | 11 | Mask attempted on the wrong layer. |
| Developing | 8 | Character exits in front of the doorway. |
| Missing | 0 | No exit. |

**This is the designed obstacle.** The background is deliberately one flat layer, so the doorway has to be manufactured. Students read this as a broken file. It is the normal condition of the job: boards, matte paintings and reference art arrive flat, and anything passing behind anything else in a flat image requires a mask.

## The planning paragraph: 15%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 15 | Answers *what would you have had to plan in the drawing to make this easier* with something specific and correct. |
| Good | 13 | Specific, but names only one thing. |
| Average | 11 | On topic and vague. |
| Developing | 8 | Generic. *"I would plan better."* |
| Missing | 0 | Omitted. |

**This is the only part that isn't button-pushing**, which is why it's worth as much as a technical step. Acceptable answers name something concrete: the doorway should have been its own layer, the arm should have been drawn clear of the torso, the background should have been drawn wider so the character could enter from off-frame.

---

## What is not graded

**Animation quality.** The walk may be a slide, the wave a metronome. No easing, arc or timing requirement.

**Drawing.** All artwork is supplied.

**Polish.** No transitions, effects or sound. It's a rig test.
