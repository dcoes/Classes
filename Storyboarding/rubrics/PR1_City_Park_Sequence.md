# PR1 City Park Sequence: Rubric
**Totals 100% of this assignment · 8% of the course grade · Band PR**
**Brief:** [`assignments/PR1_City_Park_Sequence.md`](../assignments/PR1_City_Park_Sequence.md)
**Assessed against CO1 through CO5.**

> Simple by design. This assignment assesses **continuity and staging under constraint**: one location, two characters, nothing exotic. What's being tested is whether you can keep a space coherent across a cut, not whether you can invent a world.

**Milestone chain:** beat sheet, thumbnails, rough boards, final boards. **Each milestone gates the next. A stage submitted without the preceding stage is capped at half its available percentage**, and the cap cascades.

---

## 1 . Beat sheet: 15%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 15 | Every beat in the sequence listed in order, one line each, nothing merged or skipped. Each beat carries an intended panel count. |
| Good | 13 | Complete and in order. Panel counts on most beats. |
| Average | 11 | Beats listed, some merged. Panel counts missing or applied evenly without thought. |
| Developing | 8 | A short list, closer to prose than to beats. |
| Missing | 0 | Not submitted. |

**This stage is the direct assessment of CO3**, tabulating storyboards based on certain events. It is not administrative overhead standing in front of the real assignment. It is part of the real assignment.

## 2 . Thumbnails, coverage and divergence: 14%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 14 | The whole sequence is thumbnailed, not just the interesting parts. At least two beats staged two genuinely different ways. |
| Good | 12 | Full coverage, one beat explored twice. |
| Average | 10 | Most of the sequence covered, no real alternatives. |
| Developing | 7 | Only the parts the student wanted to draw. |
| Missing | 0 | Not submitted. |

**A variant counts only if it changes shot size, camera angle, blocking, or which character the frame is on.** Redrawing the same idea more neatly is one version, not two.

## 3 . Thumbnails, the rejected option and format: 6%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 6 | Both versions submitted for each designated beat, the choice marked, and one line on what the **rejected** version did better. Delivered on the EX2 working sheet. |
| Good | 5 | Choice marked with a reason, format correct. |
| Average | 4 | Choice marked, no reasoning. |
| Developing | 3 | Alternatives present but unmarked, or panels at final-board size. |
| Missing | 0 | Neither. |

**The rejected-option line is the cheapest and most useful requirement on this rubric.** A student cannot write it without having genuinely considered both, and it moves the graded object from the drawing to the reasoning.

Exploration is what's graded here, not drawings. Every line is countable: a sheet, a count of variants, a count of marked choices, a page measurement. That is what makes it possible to grade brainstorming without grading taste.

## 4 . Rough boards, action blocked and silhouette: 13%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 13 | Character positions and movement legible in every panel. Poses read filled solid black; a stranger can name the action. |
| Good | 11 | Legible throughout, one or two poses ambiguous in silhouette. |
| Average | 9 | Blocking readable about half the time. Limbs lost against the torso. |
| Developing | 7 | Most poses don't read. The action has to be inferred from the caption. |
| Missing | 0 | Not submitted. |

## 5 . Rough boards, continuity established: 12%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 12 | The 180 degree line is chosen and held. Screen direction consistent. Eyelines match. |
| Good | 10 | Held, with one unintended break. |
| Average | 8 | Two or three breaks, or screen direction drifts in the middle. |
| Developing | 6 | Continuity is incidental rather than controlled. |
| Missing | 0 | Not assessable. |

## 6 . Final boards, continuity across the sequence: 12%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 12 | Line, screen direction, eyeline matches and match-on-action hold from first panel to last. Any crossing of the line is deliberate and identifiable as deliberate. |
| Good | 10 | Holds throughout with one soft moment. |
| Average | 8 | One clear unintended crossing. |
| Developing | 6 | Multiple crossings, no floor plan evident. |
| Missing | 0 | Not assessable. |

## 7 . Final boards, composition: 10%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 10 | Figure and ground separate in every panel. One dominant element. Correct headroom, lookroom and leadroom. Consecutive panels contrast. |
| Good | 9 | Strong throughout, a few panels where two elements compete. |
| Average | 7 | Figure and ground merge in several panels. |
| Developing | 5 | Mid-value figures on mid-value grounds throughout. |
| Missing | 0 | Not assessable. |

**Figure and ground separation is the number one reason student panels fail, and it has nothing to do with drawing ability.** Squint at the work. If the subject disappears, fix the value, not the drawing.

## 8 . Final boards, notation and terminology: 8%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 8 | Shot sizes and angles named correctly. Camera arrows distinguished from motion arrows. Panels numbered by the H1 convention. |
| Good | 7 | Correct with one or two loose terms. |
| Average | 6 | Notation present, terminology imprecise. |
| Developing | 4 | Camera and motion arrows used interchangeably. |
| Missing | 0 | No notation. |

## 9 . Final boards, completeness and silhouette: 10%

| Band | % | |
|---|--:|---|
| Exemplary | 10 | Correct ratio on the EX2 presentation sheet, panel and shot numbers, action described beneath each panel. Poses read in silhouette throughout the finished set. |
| Good | 9 | Complete, one element inconsistent. |
| Average | 7 | Two elements missing across the set. |
| Developing | 5 | Panels only, minimal supporting information. |
| Missing | 0 | Not submitted. |

> **A final panel that is a scan, trace or upscale of a thumbnail is not a final panel. It scores as a missing panel.** The thumbnail's job is to be thrown away. If you're protecting one, it has stopped doing its job.

---

## Critique participation

Peer notes given during the City Park critique count as CO5 evidence and are recorded. A student who submits work and gives no notes has met CO1 through CO4 and not CO5.

---

## What is not graded

**Rendering.** Not once, anywhere on this rubric. A crude panel that stages the beat clearly outscores a polished panel that doesn't.

**Ambition.** This is a constrained brief on purpose. Adding a car chase to City Park does not earn marks and usually costs them, because continuity is harder to hold across complexity and the assignment is *about* holding continuity.
