## What changed from the course as previously taught

**Continuity is taught once and exercised twice.** Deck 08a (the rules) moves to class 4, because PR1 is graded on those rules and it is unreasonable to assess students on material they haven't had. The *strip exercise* stays at class 10 against Deck 08b, where three of its six strips are 08b material and the rest is spaced retrieval.

**Staging and Acting is new, at class 9**, replacing the old "Final Project Overview" slot. The final still launches there. Every critique note that sounds like "it's stiff" or "I can't tell what they want" is an acting note, and there was no vocabulary for it anywhere in the course.

**After Effects moves in with Animatics at class 11.** The AE exercise ran every term with no deck, no brief and no rubric. It now has all three, and it shares a class with Deck 12 rather than taking one of its own.

**The Pitch is new, at class 13**, alongside the final check. It's the strongest available evidence for the objective about explaining creative work, and it's the one assessment where a student with good instincts and shaky hands can be unambiguously excellent.

**The midterm is briefed at class 2**, not assigned late. It was never scheduled anywhere in the repo before.

## Swap-in material

Fourteen classes is tight, and one built deck plus four specced modules do not have a permanent slot. All of them are worth teaching, so they exist as swap-ins rather than being cut.

| Material | Recommended use |
|---|---|
| **Deck 10, Boarding for Games** *(built, 28 slides)* | Strongest swap for the second half of class 13, or replaces Deck 09 in a cohort that is mostly game production majors |
| [`modules/`](modules/) **New Media & Short-Form** | Swap for Deck 10. The delivery format students are most likely to be paid for first |
| [`modules/`](modules/) **AI in the Pipeline** | 45-minute insert into class 13 or 14. Never a full class |
| [`modules/`](modules/) **Previs & Virtual Production** | Swap for Deck 10, strongest for students heading toward 3D |
| [`modules/`](modules/) **VR, 360 & Immersive** | Closer for class 14, or a swap for 13 |

If the block schedule runs longer than 14 meetings in a given term, Deck 10 is the first thing to promote to a full class.
