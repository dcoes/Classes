## How this reconciles with the course as previously taught

Three conflicts, resolved:

**Continuity appears twice, and that's intentional.** Deck 08a (the rules) stays at Session 4 per `PROJECT.md` §9, because PR1 is graded on those rules and it's unreasonable to assess students on material they haven't had. The *strip exercise* runs at Session 10 against Deck 08b — three of its six strips are 08b material, and the two that aren't are spaced retrieval rather than a repeat.

**The final launches at Session 9, not Session 12.** A 200-point chain with seven milestones cannot fit into five sessions. §9 does not protect Session 12; the placement was derived, not decided. Deck 09 moves with it, since its own stated rationale is "at final-project launch."

**Lighting moves to Session 11.** §9 says later, and the redesigned lighting study now attaches to the final's key frames rather than competing with City Park for the same week.

Two gaps also close here: **the midterm was never scheduled anywhere** in the repo — its brief and rubric both said only "single submission" — and **the After Effects exercise had no deck at all** despite running every term.

Session 15 is the designed swap point. Specs live in [`modules/`](modules/); each is a standalone 12–18 slide block.

| Module | Best used |
|---|---|

| **Previs & Virtual Production** | Substitutes for Deck 10; strongest for students heading toward 3D |
| **VR, 360 & Immersive** | Closing argument in S16, or a swap for S15 |
