# Modules: swap-in blocks

Four standalone blocks, 12–18 slides each. None is a required session. Each names a recommended host and can be run, swapped, or skipped without disturbing anything else in the course.

**Why modules rather than sessions.** Fourteen classes are already spoken for. These four topics are all worth teaching and none of them is worth displacing continuity, composition or the final. Building them as blocks means the course can flex by cohort (a room of game majors gets Deck 10, a room of film majors gets New Media, and a room heading into 3D gets Previs) without rewriting the schedule each term.

| Module | Slides | Host | Best for |
|---|--:|---|---|
| [New Media & Short-Form](M1_New_Media_and_Short_Form.md) | 16 | class 13, replacing Deck 10 | Cohorts with no game majors. The delivery format students are most likely to be paid for first. |
| [AI in the Pipeline](M2_AI_in_the_Pipeline.md) | 12 | 45-min insert into class 13 or 14 | Any cohort. **Never a full session**: see the file. |
| [Previs & Virtual Production](M3_Previs_and_Virtual_Production.md) | 16 | class 13, replacing Deck 10 | Students heading toward 3D, VFX, or games. |
| [VR, 360 & Immersive](M4_VR_360_and_Immersive.md) | 14 | class 14 as a closer, or class 13 | Any cohort. Works as the course's closing argument. |

## What they have in common

Every one of these modules exists to make the same point from a different direction: **shot grammar is a system, not a film convention.** Each takes the vocabulary built across the term and runs it against a medium it wasn't designed for, and watches what survives.

That is the `CLAUDE.md` §1 argument delivered as evidence rather than assertion, and it is why these are worth teaching to animation and film majors who think they aren't the audience.

They also share a structural rule, inherited from `PROJECT.md` §9: **no tool-specific workflow content.** Unreal, Cinemachine, Midjourney, Runway and the rest may be named. None of them gets taught. The moment a module becomes a software tutorial it has stopped doing its job and should be cut back.
