# A1 — Template Build
**50 points · Assigned Session 2 · Due Session 3**
**Rubric:** [`rubrics/A1_Template_Build.md`](../rubrics/A1_Template_Build.md)

---

## The assignment

Build the storyboard template you will use for every other assignment in this course.

You will be given an aspect ratio. Build a page of panels at that ratio, with room underneath each panel for the information a board has to carry.

That's it. It is a rectangle with the right proportions and some labeled space.

---

## Why this is assignment one

Before you compose anything, before you stage anything, before you decide where the camera goes — you have to know the shape of the frame.

If your template is the wrong shape, every panel you draw this month is composed for a frame that doesn't exist. Every headroom decision, every leadroom decision, every relationship between a subject and an edge gets recalculated the moment the ratio changes. You would have to redraw all of it.

So: fifty points for getting a rectangle right. Take them.

---

## The arithmetic

**Frame height × aspect ratio = frame width.**

At a 4-inch panel height:

| Ratio | Width |
|---|---|
| 1.85 : 1 | 4 × 1.85 = **7.4″** |
| 2.39 : 1 | 4 × 2.39 = **9.56″** |
| 16 : 9 (1.78 : 1) | 4 × 1.78 = **7.12″** |
| 1.33 : 1 | 4 × 1.33 = **5.32″** |

Going the other way — if you know the screen dimensions and want the ratio: **width ÷ height.** A 4 × 3 screen is 4 ÷ 3 = 1.33 : 1.

We work through this live in Session 2. Bring a calculator or a phone.

## What to deliver

1. **Layered source file** — `.psd`, or any format that preserves layers and opens in Photoshop or Photopea
2. **Flattened export** — `.png` or `.jpg` at the correct pixel dimensions
3. Both named per the convention in **H1**

Post both to Canvas before Session 3 begins.

---

## What each panel needs room for

| | |
|---|---|
| **Panel / shot number** | `SEQ 01 / SC 04 / SH 010` |
| **Shot size and angle** | `MS, low angle` |
| **Action description** | One line, present tense |
| **Camera notation** | Space for arrows and move labels |

Look at **H1** for the notation you're leaving room for. Don't guess at the layout — you'll be filling this in for four weeks and the fields you skip now are the fields you'll be cramming into margins later.

---

## Software

Photoshop or **Photopea** (free, runs in a browser, opens and saves `.psd`).

If you have never opened Photoshop, say so. The Photoshop prerequisite is not reliably enforced and you will not be the only one. There is a demo in Session 2 and I would rather spend ten minutes on it than have you lose fifty points to a file format.

---

## How this is graded

On whether the document is **correct and usable**. Nothing here is graded on drawing, because there is no drawing in it.

The most common way to lose points is having the ratio "about right" instead of measured. Show your arithmetic — partial credit is available for an arithmetic slip, not for a guess.

The second most common way is getting width and height backwards. Check which number is which.
