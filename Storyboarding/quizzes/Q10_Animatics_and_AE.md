- Panel rendering
- Transition variety
- Resolution
> Source: PR3 brief and rubric.

### Q10.03 · MC
Two animatics both feel wrong. One is too long, one is too short. In a submission they:
- * Look identical, and have opposite fixes
- Look obviously different
- Are both fixed by adding music
- Are both fixed by cutting panels
> Source: PR3 brief and rubric, the failure this assignment catches.

### Q10.04 · MC
You pick a frame rate at the start of an After Effects project because changing it later:
- * Shifts every keyframe you have already set
- Changes the comp resolution
- Requires re-importing the artwork
- Alters the aspect ratio
> Source: H8. Deck 13, slide 14.

### Q10.05 · MC
Panels are named `SEQ01_SH010`, `SEQ01_SH020`, incrementing by ten. Why by ten?
- * So you can insert a shot later without renaming everything downstream
- So the files sort correctly
- To match the shot list numbering in the script
- Because Canvas requires three digits
> Source: H8. H1. PR3 rubric.

### Q10.06 · MC
Importing a layered `.psd` into After Effects, the correct setting is:
- * Import As: Composition, Retain Layer Sizes
- Import As: Footage
- Import As: Composition
- Import As: Sequence
> Source: Deck 13, operation 1. H8.

### Q10.07 · MC
Getting that import setting wrong is easy to miss because:
- * It is invisible until you try to move something
- It throws no error at any point
- It only affects the final render
- The layers look correct in the timeline either way
> Source: Deck 13, slide 4. H8.

### Q10.08 · MC
In After Effects, the anchor point is best understood as:
- * The joint
- The centre of the layer
- The origin of the composition
- The point the camera looks at
> Source: Deck 13, operation 2. H8.

### Q10.09 · MC
An arm parented to a body, with its anchor point left at the layer's centre, will:
- * Rotate around its own middle like a propeller
- Fail to follow the body
- Rotate correctly from the shoulder
- Refuse to accept keyframes
> Source: Deck 13, slide 6. EX7 rubric, step 2.

### Q10.10 · MC
When a layer is parented, its own position values become:
- * Relative to the parent
- Locked and uneditable
- Absolute in composition space
- Copied from the parent
> Source: Deck 13, slide 9. H8.

The EX7 background is a single flat layer containing the house. To make the character pass behind the doorway you must:

### Q10.14 · MC
You plan a pan across a panel. What does the artwork need?
- * To be drawn wider than the frame
- To be drawn at higher resolution only
- To be split onto more layers
- Nothing, the software extends it
> Source: H8. PR3 brief, panel prep.

### Q10.15 · MC
Scratch dialogue recorded badly on a phone is required rather than optional because:
- * Timing needs a voice to time against
- The rubric requires audio of any kind
- It demonstrates effort
- Silent animatics will not export
> Source: PR3 brief and rubric, sound. H5.
