### Q2.04 · MC
2.35 : 1 and 2.39 : 1 both describe anamorphic scope. What is the difference?
- * 2.35 was the standard until SMPTE revised the aperture in 1970; 2.39 has been standard since
- 2.35 is for film and 2.39 is for digital projection only
- 2.39 is a European standard and 2.35 is American
- They are two names for exactly the same measurement
> Source: Deck 02, widescreen history. EX2 brief, "about that third number."
