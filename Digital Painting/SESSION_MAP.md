## Milestone chains

**P1 The Set Extension.** Briefed 4 · blockout due 6 · rough due 7 · final due 8.
Four class days from brief to final, which is why the brief hands out the plates
rather than asking students to find them. EX4 exists so that students who want to
shoot their own have a path, not so that everyone has to.

**P2 The Material Set.** Briefed 9 · albedo pass due 12 · final due 13. The Maya
mirror sits at 12 on purpose, late enough that there is something to look at and
early enough that there is a day left to fix what the viewport revealed.

**P3 The Extended Image.** Briefed 14 · layer plan due 15 · rough due 18 · final due
19. The Extension tier on this one is the After Effects move, and it is the only
place in the course where After Effects touches a grade.

## The machine beats

The recurring ten-minute segment, marked ● above. Each is tied to that day's
technique and each names its non-generative equivalent. See `PROJECT.md` §4.

| Session | The beat |
|--:|---|
| 2 | Generative fill as an extension of masking, and where its edges fail. |
| 5 | A generated element used as a plate. Provenance, licensing, what can be delivered. |
| 7 | The texture of generative error: perspective drift, light logic, edge mush. |
| 10 | Generated tiles, and why the seam betrays them. |
| 12 | Generated maps against painted maps, judged in the viewport rather than in the thumbnail. |
| 15 | Generated frames versus a real camera move, and why consistency over time is the hard part. |
| 16 | Consolidation. Prompting treated as art direction rather than as tricks. |
| 17 | The economics. Ownership, independence, and what is actually worth being concerned about. |

## Software, and where it is allowed

| Application | Sessions | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Photoshop** | 1 to 19 | The course. |
| **Photopea** | Named in 1, demonstrated in 2 | The home equivalent, so nobody is blocked by a license. |
| **Firefly / generative fill** | The machine beats | Already installed. No other generative tooling is required anywhere. |
| **Maya** | 11, 12, 13 | Mirror only. Provided prop, provided UV layout. No modeling, no unwrapping. |
| **After Effects** | 14 (file prep), 15 | Five operations, Extension tier. Motion Graphics owns After Effects as a craft. See `CURRICULUM.md`. |
| **Illustrator** | Optional, mentioned in 14 | Not required by any tier. |

## If the block runs short

If a day is lost to a holiday or a closure, the compression order is: merge 10 into
11 first, then merge 6 into 5. Do not compress 8, 13, 18 or 19, and do not cut 17.
The critique days are where most of the actual assessment conversation happens, and
17 is the session students will still be thinking about in a year.
